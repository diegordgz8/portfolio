### The Challenge

The client needed to turn an in-person academy into a scalable e-learning platform. The main challenge was integrating **MasterStudy LMS** with custom payment flows while keeping the user experience smooth despite heavy visual content.

### Technical Implementation

- **Core development:** Used **WordPress** as the foundation, extending the theme with **PHP** through custom hooks in the WooCommerce checkout process.
- **Design:** Built dynamic interfaces with **Elementor** and optimized loading with custom **Crocoblock** widgets.
- **Data management:** Structured Custom Post Types to handle students' downloadable resources securely.

### Results

- 40% reduction in load time through asset optimization.
- Fully automated student enrollment after purchase.
