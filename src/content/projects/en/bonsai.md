### The Challenge

Replicate the intricate logic of Bonsai's pricing page, focusing on interactivity and pixel-perfect design accuracy. The challenge was managing the pricing state (Monthly vs. Yearly) efficiently.

### Technical Implementation

- **Modern frontend:** Built with **Tailwind CSS** for fast UI development and **Alpine.js** to handle the pricing toggle logic without the weight of a full framework.
- **Architecture:** Reusable component structure in **Astro**, so each pricing card is a clean, easy-to-maintain instance.
- **Animations:** Native Tailwind transitions to improve perceived speed when switching plans.

### Takeaways

- Mastered Alpine's `x-model` directive to keep multiple UI states in sync.
- Improved accessibility of selection controls (toggles).
